# Scientific writing and optimization rules

Apply these only after correctness risks at the same location are understood.

## Scientific validity

- Keep definitions, assumptions, symbols, spaces, indices, matrix actions, units, and quantifiers consistent.
- Distinguish necessity, sufficiency, equivalence, existence, uniqueness, local behavior, and global behavior.
- Match theorem and algorithm conclusions to their stated domain and premises.
- Separate implementation verification, numerical convergence, and validation against independent evidence.

## Argument architecture

- Give each section a clear argumentative job and make dependencies visible.
- Introduce assumptions before use; connect methods to the Claims they support.
- Keep Abstract and Conclusion within the scope of the body and ledger.
- Remove duplicated contribution statements unless repetition aids navigation.

## Scientific communication

- Load and apply `$humanizer` for language, paragraph logic, and transition review. Use its embedded mode internally; return the harness's structured Issue/Unit output rather than its ordinary prose report.
- Prefer precise verbs and bounded statements over promotional language.
- State what was measured or proved, under which conditions, and with what evidence.
- Preserve established field terminology; do not trade precision for stylistic variety.
- Preserve the information rather than the existing sentence/paragraph shape, but never introduce or remove facts, numbers, citations, formulas, qualifiers, or symbol meanings.
- Report uncertainty, baselines, controls, limitations, and negative results where they affect interpretation.

## Production quality

- Check cross-references, citations, equation overflow, table width, figure legibility, caption sufficiency, float order, page breaks, and font consistency in the compiled PDF.
- Treat successful compilation as a prerequisite, not evidence of scientific correctness.
